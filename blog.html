A spoiler-safe completion log starts with a source and a save point

Keep chapter, scene type, choice context, and a clean pre-choice save in separate fields. Do not name an ending or route before a primary source or a reproducible, clearly labelled player record supports it. For current store status, compare the Steam listing with its official Steam news feed, then date the note. Continue with the CG checklist and route research guide.
